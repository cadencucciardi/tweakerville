# TweakerVille

A free co-op outbreak shooter on real Seattle and Puget Sound streets: hold the line in Survival, take the city back in Liberation, or get in and get out in Extraction. Solo or with friends.

**Website and download: https://tweakerville.pages.dev**

[Download the latest version](https://github.com/cadencucciardi/tweakerville/releases/latest):

- **Windows** (Windows 10 or 11): `TweakerVille-Setup-x.y.z.exe`. New versions show up on the home screen with an Update now button.
- **Mac** (macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel): `TweakerVille-x.y.z-macOS.dmg`. Drag TweakerVille into Applications. The first time, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Mac and PC players can play together on the same version. Maps download inside the game.
